Why is Bcrypt Industry Standard for Password Security?

🧂

Automatic Unique Salting

Bcrypt generates a cryptographically random 128-bit salt for every password. Even if two users choose password123, their final hashes are completely distinct, nullifying pre-computed Rainbow Table attacks.

🐢

Key Stretching (Slow by Design)

Fast hashes like MD5 or SHA256 allow attackers to try billions of guesses per second on modern GPUs. Bcrypt is intentionally compute-intensive, slowing down brute-force attempts exponentially.

⚙️

Adaptive Work Factor

As computing power increases over time, the salt rounds parameter (cost factor) can be increased seamlessly to maintain security strength into the future.
